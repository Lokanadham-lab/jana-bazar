# JANA BAZAR V22 Feature Coverage

## Marketplace
Amazon/Flipkart/Meesho-style catalogue foundation; Farmers, FPO, Homemade, Kirana, Local Manufacturer, Brand, National/Regional Distributor, Dealer, Wholesaler, Retailer, Service Provider, Artisan, SHG/Cooperative.

## Customer
Signup/login, OTP, recovery, addresses, search, categories, product details/specifications, cart, wishlist, checkout foundation, orders, support, returns/disputes, multilingual and location.

## Seller
Seller profile, business registration, KYC fields, GST/PAN/FSSAI/Udyam, bank verification fields, catalogue, inventory, orders, advertising, analytics/settlements architecture.

## B2B
RFQ, quotes, wholesale catalogue and purchase-order workspace foundation.

## Services
Provider profiles, service catalogue, bookings, availability, ratings/support architecture.

## Utilities
Recharge/bills, FASTag, LPG, JANA Wallet interfaces; provider-gated real transaction architecture.

## Delivery
Partner onboarding, zones, assignment, accept, pickup/delivery OTP, tracking events, COD, earnings, payouts, incidents/support schema.

## Admin
RBAC, seller/KYC approval, catalogue, orders, payments/wallet, delivery, services, support, CMS, security, audit.

## Security
RLS, server-only service role, admin bootstrap, session persistence, OTP/recovery, audit/security events, device records, idempotency, webhook records, feature flags.

## AI/Voice
UI hooks and provider-safe configuration; production provider credentials are intentionally not embedded.
